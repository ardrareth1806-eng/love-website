To My Favorite Chigglet ❤️

If you are reading this, then you have reached one of the most precious pieces of my heart. I wanted to create something that would always remind you how deeply you are loved, especially on the days like this, when we are miles apart.

Every morning, I wake up hoping your day is beautiful. Every night, I fall asleep wishing I could be beside you instead of simply saying goodnight through a screen.

Sometimes distance feels unfair, but then I remember something important. Love isn't measured by kilometers. It's measured by effort, trust, patience, and choosing each other every single day.

Thank you for making me laugh when I needed it the most. Thank you for listening to my random stories, celebrating my little victories, and making ordinary days feel extraordinary.

No matter how busy life becomes, no matter how difficult some days are, you will always be the person I choose first. My safe place. My biggest smile. My favorite hello.

One day this distance will just be a memory we'll tell people about. We'll laugh about the late-night calls, the countdowns, and how excited we were for every reunion.

Until that day arrives, please keep this little gift close to your heart because every word on it was written with love, and every click is another reminder that someone out there is thinking about you every single minute of every single day.
